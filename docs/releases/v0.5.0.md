BlindSpot's first signed release. It covers everything from v0.2 to v0.5: the tool, the fault files,
the proposed rules, and every raw result behind the numbers below.

BlindSpot breaks a disposable Kubernetes cluster in one real way at a time, confirms the harm with
probes outside the cluster, and records whether kube-prometheus-stack 92.22.0 delivered an alert,
fired one that reached nobody, only started waiting, or did nothing. Each fault ran five times, each
time on a fresh cluster. Every raw result is in `results/`, and the
[results page](https://aymen-nasri.github.io/blindspot/) is built from them.

## What the default rules did

In all, 7 of 18 faults caught.

- **Common app failures** (`results/v0.4`): all 3 caught, 5 of 5 each, after about 16 minutes.
- **Harder infrastructure failures on kind** (`results/v0.2`): 1 of 10 caught. CPU throttling fired
  and was muted by `InfoInhibitor`; DNS, packet loss, the MTU black hole, a NetworkPolicy block and a
  broken admission webhook went unseen or only started waiting; with Alertmanager or Prometheus down,
  nothing could be delivered.
- **Node failures on real VMs** (`results/v0.3`, `v0.3.1`, `v0.3.2`): disk full and inodes caught 5
  of 5, after about 16 minutes; the kubelet stopping caught 4 of 5, after about 10.
  "containerd stopped, then the app crashes" fired but delivered nothing in 5 of 5.
  Memory pressure hurt the app in 5 of 5, and `NodeMemoryHighUtilization` only ever started waiting.
- **Alertmanager's placement:** in all 6 trials where a node failure hit the node Alertmanager ran on,
  nothing was delivered after about 9 minutes. In all 4 where it ran elsewhere, the alert arrived.
  See `docs/investigations/kubelet-placement.md`.
- **False alarms:** an hour of normal work, five times, delivered nothing.

## What the proposed fixes did (Profile C)

Five rules and synthetic probes, in `rules/blindspot.rules.yml`, each with a runbook. All five pass
all four gates: `promtool` and `pint`, a `promtool` unit test, catching their fault in at least 4 of 5
trials, and a silent quiet test. Together they catch the 7 faults they were written for, all of which
the defaults missed:

| Fault | Defaults | With the fixes |
|---|---|---|
| Packet loss, MTU black hole, NetworkPolicy block | not delivered | caught 5 of 5 each, about 3 min |
| Admission webhook down | not delivered | caught 5 of 5, about 6 min |
| DNS very slow | not delivered | caught 5 of 5, about 6 min |
| Upstream DNS returns NXDOMAIN | not delivered | caught 4 of 5, about 8 min |
| CPU throttled | muted | caught 5 of 5, about 16 min |

The NXDOMAIN rule started its wait during cluster setup in 7 of 60 trials, which the quiet test
doesn't see. Tune it for your own cluster.

Not yet tested: spreading Alertmanager across nodes, the fix the placement finding points to.

## Changes made after seeing results

Each was written into the method docs before the rerun it led to, and the original results are kept:

- The DNS faults' CoreDNS block wasn't counted in metrics, so no CoreDNS rule could see them. Fixed
  and rerun (`docs/method-v0.5.md`).
- Memory pressure did no harm in its first two runs. The first change kept `stress-ng`'s OOM
  protection, which wasn't enough; the second sized it from `MemAvailable` instead of free memory, and
  was declared the last change before it ran (`docs/method-v0.3.md`).
- containerd stopped, even during a rollout, did no harm: running pods kept serving. The fault became
  "containerd stopped, then the app crashes" (`docs/method-v0.3.md`).
- The rollout undo failed when a rollout never made a second revision. Every patch is now undone by
  restoring the saved pod template.

## Checks

- 235 trials in Git, and all 50 control trials passed, on both labs and under Profile C.
- 152 tests, ruff, `promtool` and `pint` in CI on every push.
- Gitleaks, Trivy and CodeQL on every push; Dependabot weekly; a weekly regression run that opens an

## Verify this release

Every file is signed with Cosign, keyless, by this repository's release workflow, and the SBOM
(`blindspot-sbom.cdx.json`, CycloneDX) lists exactly what the package installs. To check a file:

```bash
cosign verify-blob --bundle blindspot-0.5.0-py3-none-any.whl.sigstore.json \
  --certificate-identity https://github.com/aymen-nasri/blindspot/.github/workflows/release.yml@refs/tags/v0.5.0 \
  --certificate-oidc-issuer https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com \
  blindspot-0.5.0-py3-none-any.whl
```

## Limits

Eighteen faults, a small app, a kind cluster and a three-node VM cluster on CI runners. The faults
weren't picked at random, so none of these numbers is a catch rate for failures in general. Nothing
has run on a production cluster, and it must not.
